# Security and privacy reporting

AegisQDA authorizes synthetic material only and makes bounded process claims,
not guarantees of anonymity or valid qualitative interpretation.

Report reproducible defects to the maintainer through an agreed private channel.
Use invented examples only. Never submit research sources, mappings, keys, run
bundles, protected-review screenshots or identifying outputs. Public issues
should contain only minimal synthetic examples, detector-pack version, expected
behavior and observed gate status.

No fixed response-time service level is promised. Remediation and publication
depend on verified evidence and maintainer availability. Notices describe scope,
affected detector identities, mitigations and limitations without protected data.

Legacy recognizer packs v1/v2 require a fresh scan and human review. `audit` is
diagnostic; even a finding-free legacy audit does not authorize a release.
See [the wave-1 review](docs/GENERAL_REVIEW_WAVE1.md).
